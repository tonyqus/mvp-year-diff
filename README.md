# Data Diff Between Yearly Data of Microsoft MVP program

## 2025 vs 2026

The dataset compares active Microsoft MVP counts across 109 countries and regions between 2025 and 2026. Overall, the MVP program experienced modest net growth, rising from 3,702 to 3,774 MVPs (+72 members, +1.94%).

### Macro Distribution Trends

- Growth: 53 countries/regions expanded their MVP base.
- Decline: 22 countries/regions experienced net losses.
- Unchanged: 34 countries/regions maintained the exact same MVP count.

### Key Gainers and Decliners

- Strongest Growth in Western Europe: The Netherlands posted the largest absolute gain across the entire program (+24 MVPs, +11.8%), followed by Switzerland (+12, +17.4%), Spain (+12, +9.4%), Italy (+9, +11.5%), and Denmark (+8, +12.5%).
- Contraction in East Asia: The largest single decline occurred in China, which dropped by 47 MVPs (-33.6%, falling from rank 8 to rank 13 globally). Other notable declines in the region include Korea (-13, -21.0%) and Taiwan (-9, -19.1%).
- Emerging Markets Surge: Tunisia grew from 1 to 4 MVPs (+300%), Saudi Arabia grew from 4 to 9 (+125%), and the United Arab Emirates expanded from 5 to 9 (+80%).
